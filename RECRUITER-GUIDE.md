# Use the recruiter in your own ChatGPT chat

The Media, Tech & AI Executive Recruiter started as a public custom GPT. While the replacement plugin is private, these starter prompts let you do three of the same kinds of career work in an ordinary ChatGPT conversation. They’re built for sales, partnerships, and go-to-market roles across media, tech, ad tech, and AI.

Open a new chat, copy the prompt for the task you want, and replace the bracketed parts. Start with a short, redacted excerpt. Remove contact details and keep confidential client, employer, and candidate information out of the chat. For compensation, rounded or fictional figures can be enough to explore the tradeoffs.

## 1. Get a recruiter’s read on a role

Copy this when deciding whether to apply or what experience to lead with.

```text
Help me decide whether to pursue this role and how to describe my fit.

Job description: [Paste the relevant requirements.]
My background: [Add three to six bullets covering your scope, results,
and what you personally owned.]

Start with a two-sentence recruiter read. Identify my strongest evidence,
the biggest gap, and one practical next step. Separate requirements
stated in the posting from your inferences. Point out where my evidence
is unclear, including team results I can't claim as my own. Suggest a
positioning sentence that uses only facts I've supplied.
```

## 2. Practice a real interview

Copy this when you want an interviewer to ask follow-ups as you answer.

```text
Run a mock interview for this role: [Paste the role and interview stage.]
My relevant background: [Add a few facts about your experience.]

Ask one question at a time and wait for my answer. After each answer,
briefly tell me which part was convincing and what evidence is missing.
If I am vague, ask one follow-up based on an example I actually mentioned.
Help me make the answer more specific without adding accomplishments or
experience. For an AI role, distinguish personal use, a demo or pilot,
and a deployment I actually helped deliver.
```

## 3. Compare two offers

Copy this when the headline compensation numbers are hard to compare.

```text
Help me compare these two offers using the terms I know.

Offer A: [Base, target bonus or commission, guarantees, sign-on, and
equity terms if available.]
Offer B: [Same fields.]
My priorities: [For example: predictable pay, work scope, or upside.]

Show guaranteed cash, variable pay, one-time payments, and equity
separately. Show the math and label any assumptions. If the payout rules
are missing, mark actual commission as unknown and make any linear
payout example explicitly hypothetical. Leave equity unvalued if the
terms are insufficient. Finish with the three questions most likely to
change my decision.
```

Use the result to decide what to verify or ask next. Review any draft before sharing it, and check current openings on the employer’s own careers site.
