# Privacy policy for Media & Tech Exec Recruiter

Gregory Lichtenthal publishes Media & Tech Exec Recruiter. This policy takes effect when it is published for the plugin.

## Using the plugin

The plugin runs inside OpenAI products. OpenAI handles prompts and files you share under the terms, privacy policy, and settings that apply to your account. The plugin has no publisher-operated server, analytics, or collection endpoint. It does not send your chats or uploaded files to the publisher. See [OpenAI's privacy policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/).

## Feedback, help, and privacy requests

The project uses Tally to receive your request category, the message you submit, and your email address if you choose to provide it. The publisher uses this information to respond to requests, diagnose problems, and improve the plugin. Please use a fictional or redacted example and leave out resumes, offer letters, and other private documents.

Tally processes and stores the form submission. If you provide an email address and receive a reply, the email providers involved process that correspondence. The publisher does not sell this information or use it for marketing, advertising, or model training. See [Tally's privacy notice](https://tally.so/help/privacy-policy).

## Retention and your options

The publisher deletes the submission and any related correspondence under the publisher's control within **60 days after resolution**, unless longer retention is necessary to comply with law or address an active dispute. Feedback is resolved once it has been reviewed and any next action recorded. The publisher removes deleted submissions from Tally's recoverable Trash by the same deadline. Service providers may have separate backup or deletion cycles.

Use the [Feedback & Help page](SUPPORT.md) to ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you shared with the publisher. Include a way to reach you if a reply or verification is needed. For data in your OpenAI account, use OpenAI's privacy controls or contact options.
