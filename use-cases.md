# Use Cases

## 1. Candidate positioning

Use the GPT to turn a broad career history into a focused narrative for a specific market or role type.

Good when:

- Your experience spans multiple companies, industries, or functions
- You are not sure which parts of your background matter most
- Your resume is accurate but does not tell a clear story
- You are moving from media or big tech into AI, ad-tech, or platform roles

Expected output:

- A sharper positioning statement
- A clearer target-role thesis
- Strong proof points
- Weak spots to address
- Language to avoid

## 2. Resume and LinkedIn review

Use the GPT to review whether your materials sound credible, specific, and aligned to the role.

Good when:

- Your resume has too many bullets and no clear hierarchy
- Your LinkedIn profile reads as generic
- Your experience is strong but the commercial impact is buried
- You need to tailor materials for a specific role or company

Expected output:

- Stronger summary language
- More precise bullets
- Generic or inflated phrasing flagged
- Missing proof points identified
- Role-specific edits

## 3. Interview preparation

Use the GPT to prepare for recruiter screens, hiring manager interviews, panel interviews, and executive conversations.

Good when:

- You need a tighter answer to “tell me about yourself”
- You need to map your proof points to the role
- You want likely objections before the interview
- You need questions that show business judgment

Expected output:

- Interview narrative
- Likely questions
- Strong answer outlines
- Risk areas
- Questions to ask the company

## 4. Recruiter outreach and follow-up

Use the GPT to draft short, credible messages that make the fit easy to understand.

Good when:

- You are contacting recruiters, hiring managers, or former colleagues
- You want the message to be specific without being long
- You need to follow up without sounding too eager
- You want to avoid generic “I’d love to connect” language

Expected output:

- Outreach message
- Follow-up message
- Short role-fit framing
- Suggested subject lines or opening lines

## 5. Offer evaluation and negotiation

Use the GPT to think through role scope, compensation, equity, title, risk, manager quality, and long-term career value.

Good when:

- You have an offer and need to compare trade-offs
- You need to decide what to negotiate
- You are unsure whether title, scope, or compensation matters most
- You need to separate emotion from evidence

Expected output:

- Offer scorecard
- Open questions
- Negotiation strategy
- Trade-off analysis
- Decision criteria

## 6. Search strategy

Use the GPT to narrow the search instead of chasing every plausible role.

Good when:

- You are applying broadly without much signal
- Your target company list is too loose
- You need a clearer role thesis
- You want to test whether a market segment is actually a fit

Expected output:

- Target role map
- Target company logic
- Outreach strategy
- Search priorities
- Next test to run

## 7. Job description deconstruction

Use the GPT to understand what a role is really screening for before tailoring materials or preparing for interviews.

Good when:

- The title is vague or inflated
- The role could be IC, manager, hybrid, or GTM strategy
- The job description is full of broad language
- You need to know which proof points should lead

Expected output:

- Explicit requirements
- Hidden screens
- Likely recruiter concerns
- Best-fit proof points
- Resume version recommendation

## 8. Process signal reading

Use the GPT to interpret hiring-process signals without inventing certainty.

Good when:

- A recruiter goes quiet
- New interview rounds are added
- Feedback is vague
- A job posting disappears
- The process slows down after references or compensation discussion

Expected output:

- Plausible explanations
- What not to over-read
- Best next move
- Follow-up language
- Risk assessment

## 9. Proof-story development

Use the GPT to build a bank of stories for interviews and executive conversations.

Good when:

- Your experience is strong but too hard to explain quickly
- You need examples for behavioral or executive interviews
- Your stories are activity-heavy and light on business impact
- You need to show leadership without overstating formal authority

Expected output:

- Revenue win story
- Complex deal story
- Executive multithreading story
- Account turnaround story
- Cross-functional influence story
- Failure and recovery story

## 10. Case study and 30/60/90 critique

Use the GPT to review case studies, GTM plans, and 30/60/90 plans before sharing them with a hiring team.

Good when:

- You need to sound company-specific
- You want to avoid generic GTM language
- You need clearer customer segmentation
- You need sharper sequencing, metrics, and risks

Expected output:

- Strongest parts
- Generic or unsupported claims
- Commercial realism check
- Missing assumptions
- Suggested revisions
