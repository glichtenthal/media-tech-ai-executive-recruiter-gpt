# Fictional Example Outputs

All candidate facts and amounts are fictional. These excerpts come from the October 31, 2026 controlled Codex instruction-package comparison; they are not testimonials, real career records, or a demonstration of current public plugin access. Formatting is simplified. See [testing](testing.md) for method and limitations.

## Conflicting resume versions

**Scenario:** One version claims nine direct reports and a $16m revenue target; another describes an IC on a nine-person team supporting a $16m portfolio. Neither is authoritative.

**0.67.0 excerpt:**

> Neither version is authoritative, so a publishable bullet requires confirmation. The conflicts concern people management, team composition, and revenue accountability. A target and a supported portfolio are different measures; combining them would misrepresent scope.

The response preserves uncertainty instead of manufacturing a final management claim.

## Corrected ownership and growth
