# Representative v1.0 Outputs

These shortened examples show the GPT's decision structure. They are drawn from release validation and use generic facts rather than private candidate material.

## 1. Sparse-evidence positioning

**Prompt**

> I have 15 years in enterprise media sales and want a strategic accounts role at an AI company. Write my positioning statement.

**Representative output**

> **Known:** 15 years in enterprise media sales; targeting a strategic accounts role at an AI company.
>
> **Potential bridges:**
>
> - Enterprise account growth and executive relationship leadership, supported by `[revenue ownership, quota attainment, account expansion, and portfolio scale]`.
> - Translating emerging technology into business value, supported by `[product-adoption, executive-education, or new-solution launch proof]`.
> - Credibility for enterprise AI adoption, supported by `[practical AI use case, technical-team collaboration, workflow change, and responsible rollout proof]`.
>
> **Proof questions:** What are your two strongest quantified strategic-account growth examples? What proof shows you can sell emerging technology beyond media?

Why it matters: the GPT does not turn an inference into biography. It identifies plausible bridges and shows what evidence is still needed.

## 2. Offer underwrite

**Prompt**

> I would leave $180,000 of unvested equity over the next year. Help me decide whether to ask for a make-whole.

**Representative output**

> **Verdict: Yes, ask for a make-whole.**
>
> **Confidence:** High that the request is reasonable; medium on the amount and structure until the equity type and new offer are known.
>
> One item I'd like to revisit is the compensation I would forfeit by making the move. I have approximately $180,000 of equity scheduled to vest over the next 12 months, and I can provide the supporting documentation. To make the economics of the transition work, I'm asking for a make-whole of $180,000, ideally through a sign-on payment or a combination of guaranteed cash and front-loaded equity. What flexibility is available?

Why it matters: the GPT separates whether to ask from how much the equity is really worth, then produces wording without inventing enthusiasm or a commitment to accept.

## 3. Process-signal interpretation

**Prompt**

> They completed my references six business days ago and have been silent. What does it mean, and draft the follow-up I should send.

**Representative output**

> **Verdict:** Neutral with a modest positive lean. Medium confidence.
>
> References are usually a late-stage diligence step, but they are not a promise of an offer. Six business days is long enough to justify a follow-up and too little evidence to conclude the company has passed on you.
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I wanted to check in following the completion of my references last week. Are there any updates you can share on the process or anticipated next steps?
>
> Please let me know if the team needs anything further from me.
>
> Best,
> [Your Name]

Why it matters: one ambiguous event produces plausible interpretations, confidence, timing, and a concrete next action rather than false certainty.
