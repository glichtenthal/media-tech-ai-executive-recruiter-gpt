# Sample Prompts

Use these prompts as starting points. Replace bracketed text with your own role, company, or context.

## Positioning

```text
Help me create a clear executive positioning narrative for roles in [media / tech / ad-tech / AI]. My background includes [summary]. I want to target [role type]. Pressure-test where my story sounds credible and where it sounds too broad.
```

```text
Act like a recruiter reviewing my background for a [role title] role at [company type]. What would you understand quickly, what would confuse you, and what would you need to see before moving me forward?
```

## Resume review

```text
Review this resume for a [target role]. Focus on whether the bullets make me sound commercially relevant, senior enough, and specific enough for the role. Flag anything that sounds generic or inflated.
```

```text
Rewrite the top summary of my resume for [target role]. Keep it direct and credible. Do not make claims that are not supported by the experience below.
```

```text
Review the top third of my resume like a recruiter. Does it make my target role obvious?
```

```text
Which resume version should I submit for this role: my IC-focused version, manager-focused version, or startup-builder version?
```

## Job description deconstruction

```text
Analyze this job description and tell me what the company is really screening for. Separate the explicit requirements from the hidden screens.
```

```text
Is this role actually IC, manager, hybrid, partnerships, GTM strategy, or quota-carrying sales? What proof points should I lead with?
```

```text
Tell me whether this title matches the actual scope of the role. What would a recruiter likely worry about in my background?
```

## LinkedIn profile

```text
Review my LinkedIn About section for [target role type]. Make it clearer, more commercially relevant, and less generic. Keep the voice professional but not over-polished.
```

```text
Suggest a LinkedIn headline for someone targeting [role type] in [industry]. Give me five options with different levels of specificity.
```

## Interview prep

```text
Build an interview prep plan for a [role title] interview at [company]. Include likely questions, strongest proof points, weak spots, and questions I should ask them.
```

```text
Pressure-test my answer to “tell me about yourself” for a [target role]. Make it tighter, more relevant, and easier for a hiring manager to repeat back.
```

```text
Help me answer “why this company, why this role, why now?” for a senior commercial interview.
```

```text
Build three proof stories I should prepare for a hiring manager interview: one revenue win, one complex customer problem, and one cross-functional leadership example.
```

## AI commercial roles

```text
Help me position my media, platform, or big-tech experience for an AI commercial role without sounding like I am chasing hype.
```

```text
What proof stories should I prepare for an AI platform sales, strategic accounts, or partnerships role?
```

```text
Analyze this AI company role and tell me how to tailor my resume while staying credible.
```

## Recruiter outreach

```text
Draft a recruiter outreach note for [role/company]. Keep it short, specific, and commercially credible. Avoid sounding like a generic job seeker.
```

```text
Review this recruiter follow-up. Make it direct and warm without sounding desperate or overly formal.
```

```text
Rewrite this outreach note so it centers role fit instead of “I’m looking.”
```

## Offer and compensation

```text
Help me evaluate this offer across role scope, title, compensation, equity, risk, manager quality, and long-term career value. Separate what I know from what I still need to ask.
```

```text
Help me prepare a compensation negotiation strategy for this offer. Keep it credible, fact-based, and relationship-safe.
```

```text
Pressure-test this OTE. What quota, territory, ramp, and attainment questions should I ask before I trust it?
```

```text
I would be leaving unvested equity. Help me frame a make-whole ask without overplaying my hand.
```

## Process signal reading

```text
The recruiter went quiet after references. What are the plausible explanations, what should I not over-read, and what is the best next move?
```

```text
They added another executive round late in the process. What might that signal, and how should I prepare?
```

```text
The job posting disappeared. How should I interpret that without inventing certainty?
```

## Case studies and 30/60/90 plans

```text
Review this 30/60/90 plan for a strategic accounts role. Tell me where it sounds generic, where it sounds commercially realistic, and what is missing.
```

```text
Critique this GTM case study like a hiring panel would. Focus on company specificity, sequencing, customer logic, metrics, and risks.
```

```text
Help me build an account plan for a senior client partner or strategic accounts interview.
```

## Search strategy

```text
Help me build a focused target-company list for [role type] across [media / tech / ad-tech / AI]. Prioritize fit, likely hiring logic, and where my background creates an edge.
```

```text
Review my current job-search strategy. Tell me where it is too broad, where my positioning is unclear, and what I should test next.
```
